The redemption rate on your loyalty program is telling you something important, and most brands aren't listening.

Low redemption isn't just a metric. It's a sign that members don't find the rewards compelling enough to bother, don't understand how to get them, or have stopped believing it's worth the effort. Any of those is a problem. All three at once, which is more common than you'd think, is an emergency.

Unredeemed points are a liability on your balance sheet and a missed engagement moment in your customer relationship. The goal isn't to make redemption hard so the liability shrinks. It's to make it easy and frequent enough that members stay active and feel the programme is worth their loyalty.

We've fixed redemption problems for programs at various stages of maturity. The diagnosis is usually faster than clients expect.
