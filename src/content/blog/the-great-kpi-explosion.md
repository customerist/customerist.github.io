The north star metric conversation is one of the most valuable things a leadership team can have, and one of the least likely to happen.

Every team has KPIs. Every team now also has an AI copilot that can generate twelve more before lunch. More dashboards, more auto-summarized insights, more numbers moving in more directions—and less agreement than ever on which one actually matters.

What most businesses are missing isn't a metric. It's alignment on the one number that, if it moved in the right direction, would tell you everything else is working.

It sounds simple. In practice it surfaces every disagreement about what the business is actually trying to do: grow fast or grow profitably, acquire new customers or retain existing ones, drive revenue now or build lifetime value. No AI model resolves that disagreement for you—it just gives you more precise ways to avoid having it.

The metric is almost secondary. The alignment you reach getting there is the point.

We facilitate these conversations for leadership teams, and it consistently ranks as some of the most useful time our clients spend together all year. If your team has more dashboards than direction, it might be time.
