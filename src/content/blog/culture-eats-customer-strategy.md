You can deploy AI agents across every workflow in your customer org and still lose to a competitor with worse tech and better culture.

Automation scales what you already do, but it doesn't fix what you actually believe about customers.

Here's the tell in 2026: your AI copilots are already learning your team's real priorities. If leadership doesn't behave like the customer matters, the systems watching you won't either.

Strategy is easy. Consistency, especially when it's inconvenient, is the actual competitive advantage.
